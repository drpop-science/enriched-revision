What to look for

The liquid directly above the heater gains energy first. Follow a parcel: it rises while warm, then gradually loses energy, slows and joins the denser return flow.

Equal-volume comparison

Same volume, different mass → different density

The wireframes are passive sampling volumes: they do not trap or create particles. Each counter is the actual number of moving simulation particles currently inside that frame, updated continuously. The warmer frame samples the expanded rising plume; the cooler frame stays well separated and samples the denser low return flow on the opposite side.
