Explain convection in a liquid

  1. Particles near the heater gain thermal energy / kinetic energy and move faster.
  2. The particles move further apart, so the heated liquid expands and becomes less dense.
  3. The less-dense warm liquid rises while cooler, denser liquid sinks to replace it.
  4. This continuous circulation forms a convection current.
